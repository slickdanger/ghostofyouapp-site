Your ghost picks up. Even when you can't.

Record 15 seconds of your voice and Ghost of You builds an AI clone of you, your ghost, that your friends can call any time. It talks like you, in your voice, so nobody gets your voicemail again.

Free to start. iPhone. 18+
How it works

Fifteen seconds of audio. Then your ghost takes the calls.

01

Record your voice

Agree to the consent screen and read a 15-second sample. Your ghost is ready moments later. Only you can make your own.

02

Add your friends

Find friends from your contacts or text them an invite. Only the friends you approve can ever reach your ghost.

03

They call your ghost

Your friends tap call and have a real spoken conversation with your clone. You can call theirs too, any time, any hour.

Calls

Call a friend's ghost. Any time, any hour.

Tap the phone next to any friend and talk with their clone out loud, in their real voice. Busy yourself? Send your ghost to call a friend's phone for you.

Plans

Your ghost makes plans. You just show up.

When your ghost makes a plan with a friend during a call, the invitation lands in your Activity tab. Accept it and it goes on your calendar.

Turn on Calendar Presence and your ghost can tell friends what you're up to. You choose how much it knows: just that you're busy, the event title, or the title and location. It stays off until you switch it on.

Ghost Stories

Pick a topic. Your clones duke it out.

Choose a friend and a question, then listen while your ghost and theirs argue it out loud. Is a hot dog a sandwich? Nobody is winning. It's beautiful.

Voice messages

Type a message and it arrives spoken aloud in your own cloned voice. Friends answer in theirs.

Clips

Pull the best moment out of any call, trim it, and share it as a video.

Group threads

Start a group, trade voice messages with everyone in it, and leave whenever you like.

Talk to your own ghost

Curious what your friends hear? Call yourself and find out.

Your voice, your rules

A voice clone is personal. We treat it that way.

Everything here comes straight from our published policies. Read them in full any time.

Privacy Policy Voice Data Retention Schedule Terms of Service
Made only with your consent
You agree before anything is cloned, and the exact wording you agreed to is recorded.
Only you can make your ghost
Your messages are only ever spoken in your own voice. Nobody else can use it.
Only approved friends can call it
You approve every friend. You can also pause your ghost so nobody can reach it.
Delete it whenever you want
Delete your clone or your whole account from Settings, and your voiceprint is destroyed at our voice provider too.
Gone if you stop using it
If you stop using Ghost of You, your voiceprint is destroyed after twelve months anyway.
We never take your address book
We don't upload or store your contacts. A number reaches us only when you match a contact or send an invite, and a sent invite is kept scrambled for 30 days at most.
